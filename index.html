Project Features

  • Text-to-image generation with Stable Diffusion
  • Positive and negative prompts, plus a “Surprise me” prompt
  • 16 selectable art styles
  • Adjustable size (1:1, 16:9, 9:16), CFG scale, steps and seed
  • Generate 1, 2 or 4 images at a time
  • Prompt history, favorites, gallery and downloads
